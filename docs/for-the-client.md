# Engagement assistant

For Pat Villaceran and CITY JAM  
30 October 2026

The desk drafts a reply in the voice of the account, scores how useful that reply is likely to be, and holds it until two testers pass it and Pat approves the exact words. Sending to Threads, Instagram, X, and LinkedIn is still off. Threadify can keep scheduling Threads posts.

**Live desk:** https://social-media-git-main-blackcoffer1.vercel.app

## How a comment moves

1. Paste a comment on the queue, or let the queue read recent public music posts from Threads.
2. The model writes the reply first. The comment and the reply then appear together in the list.
3. On Limits, save the two tester names. With those names blank, approval stays closed.
4. Each tester scores communication, engagement, and rationality, from 0 to 2. The pass mark is 5 out of 6, with no hard fail.
5. Pat approves that exact text. An edit after that needs both scores and Pat’s approval again.
6. Mock-publish records that the reply was cleared. It does not send the reply. On X and on personal LinkedIn, Pat posts the draft himself and records that he did.

A comment on someone else’s post is written to earn a reply. It carries no link. On our own post, the waitlist link is used only when they asked how to join.

## The score

The desk scores the draft from 0 to 10: relevance 2.5, conversation 2, tone 1.5, usefulness 1.5, safety 1, clarity 1, originality 0.5. The notes say which points were not given. The number is a reading of the text. It is not a count of replies, followers, or sign-ups.

## What stays on the desk

A comment you save, its reply, the tester scores, and the tester names stay in that browser after a refresh. Another browser or phone does not see them.
